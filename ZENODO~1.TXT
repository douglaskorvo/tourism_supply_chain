  "description": "<p>Notebooks and derived data documenting how a corpus of user-generated tourism reviews was retrieved from the Google Places API and how it was analysed, for a study of tourism service supply chains in ten emerging economies (51,037 reviews, 11,500 providers, 29 cities).</p><p><strong>Contents.</strong> A collection notebook documenting the retrieval protocol, which runs in a dry-run mode issuing no API calls; an analysis notebook implementing text normalisation, dictionary-based construct coding with negation handling, and logistic regressions with city fixed effects and supplier-clustered standard errors; and a derived analytical dataset of coded indicators and covariates.</p><p><strong>Data availability.</strong> The raw review corpus is <em>not</em> distributed and is <em>not</em> available on request. Google Maps Platform policies permit indefinite storage of place identifiers only; review text, author names, ratings and related Places content may not be pre-fetched, cached or retained outside the permitted exceptions. The derived dataset released here contains hashed review identifiers and study-specific provider pseudonyms, and no review text or reviewer names. The collection notebook allows an independent researcher with their own API credentials to reconstruct an equivalent corpus; re-execution does not reproduce the same corpus.</p><p><strong>Measurement status.</strong> The coding dictionaries have not been validated against independent human coding. A held-out double-coded validation protocol was prepared but had not been executed at this version. No precision, sensitivity or inter-coder agreement statistics are reported.</p>",
